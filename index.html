Badminton


Basic Rules

  • The game can be played in singles or doubles format.
  • The objective is to hit the shuttlecock over the net and into the opponent's court.
  • Players must serve diagonally and alternate sides after each point.
  • A match is typically played to 21 points, with a two-point lead required to win.
  • Players must not touch the net or cross into the opponent's side of the court.

Gameplay

  • Players take turns hitting the shuttlecock back and forth over the net.
  • The shuttlecock must not touch the ground; if it does, the point goes to the opponent.
  • Players can use various shots, including smashes, drops, and clears, to outmaneuver their opponents.
  • Communication and teamwork are crucial in doubles play.

Equipment

  • Racquet: Lightweight and made of materials like carbon fiber.
  • Shuttlecock: Made of feathers or synthetic materials.
  • Court: Divided by a net with specific dimensions for singles and doubles play.
